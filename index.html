Why PlatformOne exists

The internet got its building blocks. AI is still waiting.

Email, domains, logins. The internet settled into parts every business simply has, and everything since has been built on top of them. AI arrived faster than its foundations did. Most companies are running agents with no identity, no memory, and no record of what they touched. We build the missing parts.

What we build

The parts every AI business ends up needing.

An agent is a new kind of worker. It has to say who it is, remember what it did, and answer to someone. We build those parts, and the layer that secures them, in the same pass.

Identity for every agent.

An agent acts on your behalf, so it has to say who it is, which team it belongs to, and exactly what it is allowed to touch.

Know who is acting, always

Memory that persists.

Agents start from nothing every time. Memory gives them a durable record of what they know and what they have already done, so they compound instead of resetting.
