Privacy policy

This policy explains what PlatformOne collects when you use our website and products, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to PlatformOne and to the products we operate, including Cypher.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, work email, company, and anything you write when you request a demo or contact us.
  • Product data. When your organisation uses our products, we process the configuration, policy, and activity records needed to provide the service.
  • Technical data. Basic information your browser sends, such as device type and pages viewed, used to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the service you asked for.
  • To operate, secure, support, and improve our products.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use customer content to train third-party models. Access to customer data is limited to the people who need it to run and support the service.

Sharing

We share information with service providers who help us operate the business, and only to the extent they need it to perform that work. We may disclose information where the law requires it. Where a provider processes personal data on our behalf, they are bound by written terms.

Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service and meet our legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it. Where your organisation controls a retention period under its agreement with us, that period governs.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. If our product processes data on your employer's behalf, please contact them first; we will support them in responding.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@getplatformone.com and tell us what you need.

Terms of service

These terms govern your use of the PlatformOne website and any product made available through it, unless a signed agreement between us says otherwise. Where a signed agreement exists, that agreement controls.

Using the service

You may use the service only in line with these terms and applicable law. You are responsible for the accounts and credentials issued to your organisation, for activity carried out under them, and for the content your organisation submits.

Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to breach, probe, or disrupt the service or its safeguards.
  • Do not use the service to break the law or to infringe anyone's rights.
  • Do not resell or provide access to the service to third parties without our written agreement.

Your data and ours

Your organisation keeps ownership of the content it submits. You grant us the limited rights needed to operate and support the service. We keep ownership of the software, interfaces, and documentation we provide, and of anything we develop independently.

Availability and change

We work to keep the service available and secure, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted operation. We may change or discontinue features; where a change materially reduces a paid feature, we will give reasonable notice to the customer.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited. Any specific caps or warranties are set out in the signed agreement between us.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. Where a change is material, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for customers, give notice through the account or by email.

Questions about these terms? Email hello@getplatformone.com.

Data protection

Our products sit in the path of sensitive information, so how we handle that information is part of the product rather than an afterthought.

How data is protected

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access is role-based, granted on a need-to-know basis, and logged.
  • Administrative and configuration changes are written to an append-only audit record.
  • Environments are separated so that one customer's data is not reachable from another's.

Where data is processed

We aim to process and store customer data in the region a customer requires, and to make the location explicit in the agreement rather than leaving it to be discovered. Where a transfer across borders is necessary, we put an appropriate safeguard in place first.

Processors

We use a small set of infrastructure and support providers. Each is bound by written data processing terms, and we review the list as it changes. Customers can request the current list.

Incidents

If we become aware of a breach affecting customer personal data, we will investigate, contain it, and notify affected customers without undue delay, along with what we know and what we are doing about it.

Data processing agreements

We can enter into a data processing agreement with customers who need one, and support security reviews and questionnaires as part of procurement.

For a DPA, the processor list, or a security review, email hello@getplatformone.com.
